# Upcoming Events

## Innovation & Entrepreneurship in Medicine Series :book:

Are you interested in healthcare innovation, entrepreneurship, medical devices, startups, or learning how clinical ideas become real-world solutions? This fall, PennHealthX is offering Innovation & Entrepreneurship in Medicine, a new seminar series designed to introduce medical students to the process of identifying clinical problems, developing innovative solutions, and translating those ideas into practice. Attending this seminar series counts towards completion of your HMET certificate!

Across 12 weekly sessions, you’ll learn about:
- Identifying and validating unmet clinical needs
- Understanding patients, clinicians, and other healthcare stakeholders
- Ideation, prototyping, and minimum viable products
- Intellectual property and patenting
- Navigating the FDA and regulatory pathway
- Clinical validation and translational research
- Funding, commercialization, and pitching to investors
- Lessons and experiences from physician entrepreneurs

Sessions are highly interactive and case-based, with students working in teams throughout the series to develop a healthcare innovation and ultimately pitch their idea to win prizes! **Everyone can participate! No prior experience in entrepreneurship, business, or engineering is required. Dinner or snacks provided at every session.** Whether you already have an idea for a medical innovation or are simply curious about entrepreneurship, this is an opportunity to learn from experts, explore the entrepreneurial process, and get hands-on experience developing a healthcare innovation.

## Med Students in Health AI :computer:

PennHealthX is excited to announce a **lunch talk** with Vincent Guo and Michael Yao. Vincent and Michael are current PSOM students and PennHealthX presidents who will be **discussing their work in health AI companies** as well as the way their **dual degrees have influenced their contributions** in these companies and their academic journey as a whole. There will be a Q&A at the end, so please bring any questions about pursuing dual degrees, balancing clinical medicine with research/business, and what it's like to work in industry during medical school! There will be Kimchi House!

**Vincent Guo** is an MD/MBA candidate at the Perelman School of Medicine and the Wharton School, graduating in 2027 and applying into Internal Medicine. Before medical school, he studied biomedical engineering at Columbia and helped build and run a COVID-19 testing program at a diagnostics startup. At Penn Medicine, he has led scale-ups of Epic's clinical AI tools. This summer, he worked as a product manager at Layer Health, a clinical AI company that helps health systems surface what patients need from across the medical record.

**Michael Yao** is an MD-PhD candidate at Penn, graduating in 2027 and applying into Med-Peds. His PhD research looked at statistical methods for improving the generalizability of machine learning models, with applications in clinical decision support and AI4Science. At Penn, Michael has interned with industry labs at Microsoft, Genentech, ScaleAI, and Glass Health. He is currently a Research Scientist at Abridge, working on clinical reasoning tools for physicians and medical trainees.

## Starting a Private Practice in Dermatology with Dr. Evan Rieder 🍕
PennHealthX is excited to invite you to a **lunch talk** with Dr. Evan Rieder who will share his journey **transitioning from academic medicine to opening his very own private practice dermatology clinic in NYC**.

Two years ago, he opened his very own clinic based in Tribeca and is eager to share his journey of starting his business.
