# Upcoming Events

## Innovation & Entrepreneurship in Medicine Series :book:

Are you interested in healthcare innovation, entrepreneurship, medical devices, startups, or learning how clinical ideas become real-world solutions? This fall, PennHealthX is offering Innovation & Entrepreneurship in Medicine, a new seminar series designed to introduce medical students to the process of identifying clinical problems, developing innovative solutions, and translating those ideas into practice. Attending this seminar series counts towards completion of your HMET certificate!

Across 12 weekly sessions, you’ll learn about:
- Identifying and validating unmet clinical needs
- Understanding patients, clinicians, and other healthcare stakeholders
- Ideation, prototyping, and minimum viable products
- Intellectual property and patenting
- Navigating the FDA and regulatory pathway
- Clinical validation and translational research
- Funding, commercialization, and pitching to investors
- Lessons and experiences from physician entrepreneurs

Sessions are highly interactive and case-based, with students working in teams throughout the series to develop a healthcare innovation and ultimately pitch their idea to win prizes! Everyone can participate! No prior experience in entrepreneurship, business, or engineering is required. Dinner or snacks provided at every session. Whether you already have an idea for a medical innovation or are simply curious about entrepreneurship, this is an opportunity to learn from experts, explore the entrepreneurial process, and get hands-on experience developing a healthcare innovation.

## PennHealthX Fireside Chat on Funding, Clinical Validation, and Commercialization of Digital Health Innovations 📢

We are excited to welcome Ayushi Sinha and Daniella Cohen, who will discuss how to secure funding, generate clinical evidence, and navigate the commercialization of healthcare technologies. As always, we will have plenty of snacks and treats!

**Ayushi Sinha** is the Founder and CEO of Turmerik, an AI-powered healthcare company focused on improving clinical trial recruitment. She has held roles at Bain Capital Ventures, Microsoft, and Nines, where she worked on emerging healthcare and AI technologies. She holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and a degree in Computer Science from Princeton University.

**Daniella Cohen** is a principal at 2048 Ventures, leading the health practice. Prior to joining 2048 Ventures, she was a managing partner at Rough Draft Ventures, where she invested in student founders across the country. She holds a B.A. in Neuroscience from Princeton University.
